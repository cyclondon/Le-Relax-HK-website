01 — WHO WE ARE

Most window makers buy in their glass and hardware. We don't. Processing glass, making hinges and fabricating the finished product on one site means tighter quality control, shorter lead times and factory pricing for our partners.

Our story since 1986 →
02 — MANUFACTURING

Three capabilities. One factory.

Few window factories process their own glass. We do it alongside our own hardware line and final fabrication — so one supplier is accountable for every component in your order.

01

Glass processing

Toughened, heat-soaked, laminated, insulated, Low-E, curved, fire-rated and switchable glass — eleven processes on site.

02

Friction stays & hardware

Stainless steel window friction stays made on our own line and tested to BS 6375-2, matched to the profiles they carry.

03

Fabrication & assembly

Aluminium profiles cut, machined, glazed and assembled into finished windows, doors, enclosures, balustrades and glass rooms, then packed for container shipment.
