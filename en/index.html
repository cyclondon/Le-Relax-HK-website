01 — Why LE-RELAX

Most window companies buy in their glass and hardware.We don't. Glass processing, friction stay production and assembly all happen in our own factory, so quality is more consistent and prices are fairer. Containers arrive from Zhongshan every day, so supply is reliable.

02 — Our own factory

Three processes, one factory

Very few window factories process their own glass. We even make our own friction stays, so every part, from a sheet of glass to the finished window, has someone responsible for it.

01

Glass processing

Eleven glass processes, including toughened, heat-soaked, laminated, insulated, LOW-E, bent, fire-rated and switchable glass, all done in-house.

02

Friction stays and hardware

Our own stainless steel friction stays, tested to PCCS-FH and BS 6375-2, made to fit our frames exactly.

03

Assembly

Aluminium cutting, machining, glazing and assembly into windows, doors, shower screens, balustrades and glass rooms.
