ABOUT LE-RELAX

A family manufacturer since 1986

Founded in Hong Kong in 1986 and still run by its founder. Today all production is consolidated at BD House in Zhongshan, on a 300,000 m² site with 20 production lines.

HISTORY

Four decades of windows, doors and glass

1986Incorporated in Hong Kong under the name BD Aluminium
1987Door, window & glass processing factory established in Bao'an, Shenzhen
2005Glass processing factory established in Zhongshan
2006Bao'an factory relocated to Shajing, Shenzhen
2015All factory space consolidated into Zhongshan
2015Founding member of the Hong Kong Aluminium Windows and Doors Association
2027UK trade counter opens
MANUFACTURING

Glass, hardware and fabrication in one factory

Most window suppliers buy in their glass and hardware. We process our own glass, make our own stainless steel friction stays and fabricate the finished product on one site, so every component is made and checked under one roof. Orders are handled by our sales teams and made in Zhongshan.
