ABOUT LE-RELAX

A family business for forty years
Started in Hong Kong

LE-RELAX was founded in Hong Kong in 1986 by its founder, who still runs the business today. All production is now at BD House in Zhongshan, a 300,000 m² site with 20 production lines.

Our history

1986 to today

1986Founded in Hong Kong as BD Aluminium (專業鋁質)
1987Opened a window, door and glass processing factory in Bao'an, Shenzhen
2005Opened a glass processing factory in Zhongshan
2006Bao'an factory moved to Shajing, Shenzhen
2015Factories consolidated in Zhongshan
2015HK Aluminium Windows and Doors Association founded, with LE-RELAX as a founding member
Our own factory

Glass, friction stays and finished products, all in one factory

Most window companies buy in their glass and hardware. We process our own glass and make our own stainless steel friction stays, then assemble windows, doors, shower screens, balustrades and glass rooms in the same factory. Making every part on one site means more consistent quality and fairer prices. Our Hong Kong store staff take your order and follow it through, and our Zhongshan factory makes it.

Industry standing

Trusted by the Housing Authority, leading the industry

Approved supplier

Approved aluminium window supplier to the HK Housing Authority

Our Housing Authority windows are made to British Standards BS 6375-1 and BS 6375-2 and meet the Housing Authority's typhoon test requirements.

Industry leadership

Founding member and chairman of the HK Aluminium Windows and Doors Association

LE-RELAX is a founding member of the HK Aluminium Windows and Doors Association, and our founder serves as its chairman, helping set standards for Hong Kong's aluminium window and door industry.
