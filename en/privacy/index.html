Privacy policy

Le-Relax Aluminium Glass & Hardware Products Company Limited ("LE-RELAX") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our enquiry channels, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you contact LE-RELAX by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive the information you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email, address, site photos and measurements.

How we use it

We use this information only to answer your enquiry, prepare quotes, arrange manufacture and delivery, provide after-sales service and keep transaction records. We never sell your personal data, and we will not use it for direct marketing without your consent.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous browsing data, such as pages viewed, device type, approximate region and how often buttons like WhatsApp and phone are tapped, to understand how the site is used and improve it. Google Analytics uses cookies; you can turn cookies off in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Third-party services

This website embeds Google Maps, and WhatsApp is provided by Meta. When you use these services, their own privacy policies also apply.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data, limit access to staff who need it, and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Access and correction

Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486), you have the right to access and correct the personal data we hold about you. To do so, email us at info@le-relax.hk.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Last updated: October 2026.
