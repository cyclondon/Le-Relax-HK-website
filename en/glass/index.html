GLASS TYPES

Safety, thermal, acoustic, privacy

01

Toughened

Heated and rapidly cooled for several times the strength of annealed glass; breaks into small blunt pieces.

Typical use
Shower screens, doors, windows, balustrades
Standard
BS 6206:2009 · ANSI Z97.1:2009
02

Heat-soaked

Toughened glass heat-soak tested to screen out nickel-sulphide inclusions and cut the risk of spontaneous breakage.

Typical use
Shower screens, high-rise glazing, façades
Standard
BS EN 14179-1:2005
03

Laminated

Two or more panes bonded with an interlayer; holds together when broken and reduces noise.

Typical use
Balustrades, roof glazing, acoustic windows
04

Insulated (IGU)

Sealed double-glazed units for thermal and acoustic insulation.

Typical use
Windows, sliding doors, glass rooms
05

Low-E / Sun-E

Low-emissivity and solar-control coatings that reduce heat gain.

Typical use
West-facing elevations, conservatories
06

Curved toughened

Bent then toughened for curved designs.

Typical use
Quadrant shower enclosures, curved windows
07

Fire-rated

Glazing that resists the passage of fire for a set period.

Typical use
Fire doors, escape routes
08

Switchable

Switches from clear to opaque at the touch of a button.

Typical use
Bathrooms, meeting rooms, partitions
09

Safety film

Film-backed glass that holds fragments if broken.

Typical use
Mirrors, cabinet doors, decorative glass
10

Decorative

Etched, sandblasted, patterned and art glass to order.

Typical use
Shower screens, screens, doors
11

Float

Clear float glass from 3 mm to 22 mm, for further processing.

Typical use
All applications
