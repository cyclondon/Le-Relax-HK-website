Housing Authority windows

LE-RELAX is an approved aluminium window supplier to the Hong Kong Housing Authority. Our 38 and 50 Housing Authority windows are made to the Housing Authority specification, tested to British Standards BS 6375-1 and BS 6375-2, and fitted with stainless steel friction stays made in our own factory and certified under PCCS-FH. For bulk orders from contractors, and for window replacement in HOS and private flats.

  • Approved Hong Kong Housing Authority supplier
  • BS 6375-1 · BS 6375-2
  • PCCS-FH certified friction stays
  • Direct from our Zhongshan factory

Why LE-RELAX Housing Authority windows

01

Housing Authority approved

LE-RELAX is an approved aluminium window supplier to the Hong Kong Housing Authority, and we keep investing in testing to meet its requirements.

02

Tested to standard

Our Housing Authority windows are tested to BS 6375-1:2009 and BS 6375-2:2009, covering weathertightness, operation and strength.

Our friction stays are made in our own factory and certified under PCCS-FH, the Hong Kong window hinge product certification, for a secure fit with the frame.

Who it suits

Who chooses Housing Authority windows

Contractors and window factories

Bulk supply of Housing Authority windows, profiles and friction stays to the Housing Authority specification. Containers arrive in Hong Kong from our Zhongshan factory every day, so delivery is reliable.

HOS and private flat owners

If you want durable replacement windows with easy-to-find parts, choose Housing Authority windows. For larger windows, consider our 40 to 110 series casement windows.

Repairs after window inspection

The Mandatory Window Inspection Scheme requires private buildings aged 10 years or more to have their windows inspected regularly. If stays or sashes need replacing, we can supply the right parts.

Certificates

Product certificates

Our aluminium windows and friction stays are assessed by an independent certification body under the Hong Kong Product Conformity Certification Schemes (PCCS), with regular surveillance audits. The certificate holder, Prestige Metal And Glass Products Limited, and the manufacturer, 鎮威五金玻璃制品（中山）有限公司 (our Zhongshan factory), are both LE-RELAX companies. Tap a certificate to enlarge it. You can check a certificate's authenticity and status at www.takwin.com.hk.

FAQs

FAQs

What are Housing Authority windows?+

Housing Authority windows are aluminium windows designed and made to the Hong Kong Housing Authority specification. They are mainly used in public housing estates, and many HOS and private flat owners choose them too.

Is LE-RELAX an approved Housing Authority supplier?+

Yes. LE-RELAX is an approved aluminium window supplier to the Hong Kong Housing Authority, and our Housing Authority windows are tested to BS 6375-1:2009 and BS 6375-2:2009.

Can private or HOS flats use Housing Authority windows?+

Yes. Housing Authority windows are durable and spare parts are easy to find. For larger windows or better sound and heat insulation, you can also consider our 40 to 110 series casement windows with insulated or LOW-E glass.

How do I choose between 38 and 50 Housing Authority windows?+

It depends on the window size, floor level and position. WhatsApp the store photos of the site with rough measurements, and we will suggest the right series.

Can contractors order in bulk?+

Yes. We supply Housing Authority windows, profiles and friction stays to contractors and window factories. WhatsApp 6588 1426 for a quote.
