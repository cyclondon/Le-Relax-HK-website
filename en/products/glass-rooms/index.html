FAQ

Trade questions

Do you manufacture to measure?+

Yes. Orders are fabricated to your sizes at our own factory in Zhongshan, then packed for container shipment.

Can you supply glass or hardware on its own?+

Yes. Processed glass, friction stays and hardware are available separately for fabricators and distributors.

Can I see the products before ordering?+

Yes. Visit our showrooms in Hong Kong and mainland China, or arrange a visit to the factory in Zhongshan.
