Why LE-RELAX

Why choose LE-RELAX casement windows

01

Trusted by the Housing Authority

LE-RELAX is an approved aluminium window supplier to the Housing Authority; our Housing Authority windows are made to BS 6375-1 and BS 6375-2.

02

Our own friction stays

Friction stays are made in our own factory and PCCS-FH certified, for a firmer fit with the frame.

03

Wind- and weather-tight

Designed to the Housing Authority's typhoon test requirements, for high-rise and waterfront flats.

04

Your choice of glass

Eleven types of glass processed by LE-RELAX, including toughened, laminated, insulated and LOW-E.

FAQs

FAQs

Can it be made to measure?+

Yes. Every LE-RELAX order in Hong Kong is made to your site measurements in our Zhongshan factory, then shipped to Hong Kong for installation.

What's the fastest way to get a quote?+

Take photos of the site and WhatsApp them to your nearest store with the rough width and height, and we'll reply as soon as we can. You're also welcome to visit any of our three stores.

Is supply reliable?+

Containers arrive in Hong Kong from our Zhongshan factory every day, so restocking and order delivery are reliable.
