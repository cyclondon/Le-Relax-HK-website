FAQs

FAQs

Can it be made to measure?+

Yes. Every LE-RELAX order in Hong Kong is made to your site measurements in our Zhongshan factory, then shipped to Hong Kong for installation.

What's the fastest way to get a quote?+

Take photos of the site and WhatsApp them to your nearest store with the rough width and height, and we'll reply as soon as we can. You're also welcome to visit any of our three stores.

Is supply reliable?+

Containers arrive in Hong Kong from our Zhongshan factory every day, so restocking and order delivery are reliable.
